# Academic Abstract

Single-machine makespan minimization ($1 \mid\mid C_{\max}$) is trivial: additive completion yields $C_{\max} = \sum p_j$ (14 for 4-job benchmark) in $O(n)$ time. Adding setups $s_{ij}$ and release dates $r_j$ ($1 \mid r_j, s_{ij} \mid C_{\max}$) is strongly NP-hard via ATSP/TSPTW.

We evaluate **ALNS-WDP** (destroy-repair, sliding-window Held-Karp DP $k \in [6, 10]$, adaptive bandit weighting whose benefit was not demonstrated). On 20 benchmark instances ($n \in [4, 200]$, $\alpha \in \{0, 0.3, 0.6\}$, 30 seeds) under matched budgets (within 6%; ablation up to 1.15x), ALNS-WDP achieves lower mean gap to dual lower bound (5.47%) than SA (6.65%), GA (7.70%), and MultiStart_LS (14.89%). Differences are significant only vs GA (Holm $p=0.0146$), not SA or MultiStart_LS ($p=0.128$). Exploratory split: ALNS-WDP excels on asymmetric setups (9/9 wins vs GA, +2.7% vs SA), but trails on symmetric setups (vs SA: 3W/4T/4L; GA beats ALNS on `n100_a30_sym`). Controlled setup dominance tests ($\rho_s \in [0.06, 4.30]$, 2,400 runs) confirm ALNS-WDP widens its edge on asymmetric matrices (Holm $p < 0.0001$), while 2-opt search excels on symmetric setups. $n=200$ is singular.

Ablation shows window-DP beats No_WindowDP at $n=100$ (Holm $p < 0.05$); no benefit $n \le 50$; adaptive weights show no gain (Holm $p=1.000$). On 11 small instances ($n \le 20$), gap to optimum ranks MultiStart_LS 0.27% < SA 0.40% < ALNS-WDP 0.45% < GA 1.97%. Bi-objective tests show ALNS-WDP achieves lower IGD on 3/6 instances (Holm-significant on 1/6), while NSGA-II attains higher hypervolume on 5/6 (Holm-significant on 5/6) because Window-DP optimizes makespan only.
